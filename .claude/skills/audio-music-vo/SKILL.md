# Audio, Music & Voice Production

## Procedure
1. Read the world audio bible and event priority table.
2. Define BPM, key/mood, instrumentation, loop length, stems and transition points.
3. Create original or properly licensed music; record source/model/licence.
4. Make SFX short, distinct and non-fatiguing.
5. Give critical telegraphs a unique frequency/rhythm and visual equivalent.
6. Record robot VO in short lines, normalize loudness, trim silence, and tag priority.
7. Implement audio events through the existing audio manager.
8. Test on phone speaker, headphones and muted mode.

## Rules
- Never let VO mask a critical warning.
- No copyrighted melody imitation or unlicensed samples.
- Music loops must be seamless.
- Provide mute/music/SFX controls.
- Do not play repeated UI sounds on every frame/state refresh.

## Checklist
- [ ] Loop tested.
- [ ] SFX levels balanced.
- [ ] VO priority correct.
- [ ] Licence/provenance recorded.
- [ ] Gameplay remains readable muted.
