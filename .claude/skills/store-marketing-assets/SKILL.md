# Store Listing & Marketing Assets

## Procedure
1. Identify the actual build features; never advertise unimplemented features.
2. Define one primary hook: draw roads to survive a world that fights back.
3. Produce an icon readable at small size with one focal subject.
4. Capture real gameplay screenshots with minimal truthful captions.
5. Show road drawing, Road-Eater, robot projectiles, surfaces and vehicle collection.
6. Create a short trailer with gameplay first, not logo first.
7. Check Google Play current asset dimensions, metadata and policy requirements.
8. Localize store copy only after core English copy is approved.

## Rules
- No fake UI or fabricated gameplay screenshots.
- No misleading rewards or exaggerated multiplayer claims.
- Do not use third-party game logos or lookalike branding.
- Keep claims verifiable.
- Record rights for music, fonts, footage and artwork.

## Checklist
- [ ] Screens are real gameplay.
- [ ] Claims match release build.
- [ ] Icon reads at small size.
- [ ] Rights logged.
- [ ] Store policy checklist completed.
