Smooth catalogue (garage) vehicles, restyle of 28 of 32 vehicle items. Forged 2026-11-15. Status: forged, NOT reviewed (inspector decides).

METHOD
Procedural vector art, our own code: tools/art/make_vehicles2.py + tools/art/veh2lib.py on top of tools/art/smoothart/ (pure Python
rasteriser, 4x supersampled masks). Same recipe as the slice cars: rounded-polygon silhouettes (round_poly), DeepInk outline of 0.04
world units sized from the DISPLAY density (about 5 px), cel3 three-tone shading from the upper left, rim light, gloss, cool gradient
windows, dark arch liners, 4 px colour bleed. Wheels are drawn by one parametric function (car, sport, truck, lug, tractor, cart, wire):
tyre, tread notches, rim, dish, 4 to 8 spokes, hub in the vehicle accent colour and ONE bright bolt so rotation reads.
No generative AI, no downloads, no third-party pixels. The old Kenney / forged / OGA previews were used ONLY as a size and layout
reference (canvas, wheel anchors, silhouette box, what the vehicle is); none of their pixels are in the new files.
Regenerate: python3 tools/art/make_vehicles2.py --output <dir>   (about 25 s)   then   python3 tools/art/vehicles2_report.py <dir>

WHAT IS KEPT EXACTLY (so colliders, wheel spin and gameplay numbers do not change)
- Canvas = old preview canvas x an integer S (8 to 11, 5 for the motorcycle, 7 for the snowmobile). Pivot is the centre, as before.
- Wheel anchors (cx, cy), radius r and cut-out size = old values x S, listed in MAPPING.md and wheels_new.json. The drawn wheel has
  radius r*S (the physics radius), so the picture agrees with the collider.
- Silhouette box (outline included) fills the old content box (1 old px margin): within about 0.1 old px for most; larger deviations
  are listed in MAPPING.md (old vs new bounds).
- NOT kept: pixel content, obviously. Colours changed where the old car clashed with another (see below).

THREE FILES PER VEHICLE WITH WHEELS
 previews/<id>.png   body + wheels baked (catalogue card, and a drop-in for the old Previews/<id>.png)
 bodies/<id>.png     body only, wheel arches show a dark liner; use as the rig body so the spinning cut-outs are not drawn over baked wheels
 wheels/<id>_w<i>.png  round wheel cut-outs, size = old size x S, same file names as Assets/Art/Catalog/Wheels
Vehicles without cut-outs (handcar, moon rover, tank, snowmobile, sled, toboggan, snowboard, skateboard): bodies/ and previews/ are identical
(wheels, if any, are baked, as in the old picture).

Shared weaknesses: wheels are small on the cars because the anchors and radii are fixed by the old art (police 3 old px of a 34 px body);
the long front overhang (front wheel at about 60 to 65 percent of the body) comes from the old anchors and makes every car a long-hood
shape; shading is one fixed light direction so tall parts (bus, trailer) have a dark right end; riders (sled, toboggan, snowboard) are
simple cap + head + block torso; no decals or text anywhere (taxi sign, police) by choice.
