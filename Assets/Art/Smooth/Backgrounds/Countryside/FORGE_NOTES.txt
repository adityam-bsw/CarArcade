passes everywhere (sky 10.7, horizon 9.2, near hill 4.1). So red hazards/tells must keep the DeepInk outline or a light under-stroke
and a shape; red-only tells fail the 3:1 rule on Countryside. Atmospheric perspective holds: mean luminance far 0.55 > mid 0.33 > near 0.21.

tree edges at the wrap, visually continuous).

pale and low in contrast; cloud base is a little blocky; near trees are large (about 4 units) which may crowd the road area.
