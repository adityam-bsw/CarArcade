Smooth slice vehicles: ClassicRed, SportCyan, MonsterTruck (body + wheel as separate PNGs)
Method: procedural vector art, own code. tools/art/make_vehicles.py + tools/art/smoothart/ (sprites.py is new: Sprite helper with
outline from display density, 3-tone cel shading, upper-left rim light, gloss, round_poly fillets). 4x supersampled masks,
DeepInk outside-only outline, 4 px colour bleed. No generative AI, no downloads, no third-party pixels. Licence: own work (procedural).
Review pictures (not assets): review/assembled_*.png (body + wheels placed like RoadCar.cs does), review/scene_colour.png and scene_grey.png.

Files (all facing right, transparent background):
  body_classic.png 480x180   body_sport.png 480x160   body_monster.png 480x200   (same canvases as CarBody/SportCarBody/MonsterTruckBody)
  wheel_classic.png, wheel_sport.png, wheel_monster.png  160x160  (same canvas as Wheel.png; centre pivot)

What the game really loads (RoadCar.cs, Build(Vector2, VehicleType)): Resources/RoadBuilder/CarBody|SportCarBody|MonsterTruckBody and a
single shared Wheel. Bodies are stretched to world boxes 2.4x0.9, 2.4x0.8, 2.6x1.1 units (about 200 px per unit), wheel diameter
2*r*1.03 with r 0.42, 0.40, 0.52, wheel anchors at +-0.72/0.70/0.78 x. Collider is data (normalised polygon), not sprite-derived.
Silhouette extents match the old sprites (outer bbox classic 7..470 x 12..154, sport 7..472 x 20..142, monster 18..477 x 7..157;
old: 7..472 x 6..155, 7..472 x 13..143, 17..472 x 1..162), so the collider polygon still sits on the drawn body. Arches are centred
on the physics wheel anchors. Pivot centre, canvas sizes unchanged => no gameplay change from swapping the files in place.
NOTE: the Countryside screenshot smooth_countryside_04_asphalt.png does NOT show these three cars. It shows a pixel Kenney police
car (catalogue vehicle with cut-out wheels), so the slice vehicles (and pickups) were checked from the code path and the older
screenshot-20261028-175756.png, where the red slice car has body-high wheels exactly as assembled here.

Decisions and deviations (read before shipping):
 1. Outline is 0.04 WORLD units measured at the display density, not a fixed 5 px: 8 px on the bodies (200 px/unit), 7 px on the
    wheels. At 128 px/unit that is the Bible's 5 px; these sprites are shown at about 190-200 px/unit, so 5 px would be thinner
    (0.025 units) than the rest of the world.
 2. Three wheel sprites (classic 5 spoke steel/red hub, sport 6 spoke light alloy/cyan hub, monster knobby lugs/orange beadlock).
    The game code loads ONE shared Wheel; per-vehicle wheels need a small RoadCar change (route to implementer). Until then use
    wheel_classic.png as the shared Wheel.png (fits all three bodies, monster loses its lug silhouette). The tyre is drawn 5 percent
    smaller than the canvas (4 px bleed margin), which together with the code's 1.03 factor nets about 0.98 of the physics wheel.
 3. Rotation readability: tread notches/lugs, spokes and one bright off-centre bolt on every wheel.
 4. Classic keeps its red (VehicleType ClassicRed). Bible 6 lists RacingOrange for the Classic car and reserves SignalRed for damage.
    I used a cherry red (#D8323C, bluer than SignalRed). Open decision for the art owner: keep red, or recolour (palette tokens
    CarRed* in smoothart/palette.py). Monster stays orange, sport cyan.
 5. Each body carries a cartoon driver (as the old sprites did). Lamps: warm yellow head, red tail.
 6. Body sits behind huge wheels: at the game's rig the wheel diameter is about equal to body height, so the lower half of the body
    is hidden by design. Wheel arches (charcoal liner) show only when the suspension moves.

Quality (honest, 1 to 5; placeholder under 3):
  wheels 4 (clean, three clearly different, readable spin cues)
  body_monster 4 (strongest silhouette: tall cab, stacks, light bar, bull bar, lug wheels)
  body_classic 3.5 (reads as a car; closest to a generic sedan)
  body_sport 3.5 (low roof, spoiler, long nose; silhouette still similar to classic at tiny size, mainly the spoiler separates them)
Limits: not hand-drawn art; shading is gradient cel, no fine detail; the driver is a tiny head; no damage/invincibility variants;
no night tint test. Not verified in Unity (no import, no play test): wheels placement was checked only by the python assembly script.
Import (when promoted to Assets/Art/Smooth/Vehicles/...): SmoothArtImporter 128 ppu is WRONG for these three because RoadCar
fits them by bounds; any ppu works, but keep square wheels and the same pixel sizes. Bilinear, no mipmaps, alpha is transparency.
