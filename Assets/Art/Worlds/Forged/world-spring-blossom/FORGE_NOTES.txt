world-spring-blossom: four looping 384x216 layers (spring-blossom_0_sky, _1_far, _2_mid, _3_near), VISTA layer format.
Method: Recolour of VISTA downs layers to pastel greens, own pastel sky with clouds, drawn pink/white blossom trees, flowers and drifting petals. Made with tools/assets/pixel.py plus Python scripts. Not hand-drawn; props are drawn from coordinates in code.
Source: CC0 VISTA by najjar320 (Assets/Art/Backgrounds/Vista/downs)
Limits: Shares hill silhouette with summer and farm.
Seam scores: see tools/assets/pixel.py tileable_score; checked at build time (all layers loop by construction, sparse sharp details raise the score a little).
quality: 4 of 5
Unity import (to do in inspector): point filter, no mipmaps, uncompressed, 16 px per unit.
