world-haunted-halloween: four looping 384x216 layers (haunted-halloween_0_sky, _1_far, _2_mid, _3_near), VISTA layer format.
Method: Recolour of VISTA canopy layers to deep purples, own night sky (stars, moon, bats, cloud streaks), drawn haunted house, dead trees, tombstones, pumpkins, fences. Made with tools/assets/pixel.py plus Python scripts. Not hand-drawn; props are drawn from coordinates in code.
Source: CC0 VISTA by najjar320 (Assets/Art/Backgrounds/Vista/canopy)
Limits: Moon shading is a hard diagonal split; pumpkin faces are 3 px.
Seam scores: see tools/assets/pixel.py tileable_score; checked at build time (all layers loop by construction, sparse sharp details raise the score a little).
quality: 3 of 5
Unity import (to do in inspector): point filter, no mipmaps, uncompressed, 16 px per unit.
