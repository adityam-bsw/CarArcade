world-synthwave: four looping 384x216 layers (synthwave_0_sky, _1_far, _2_mid, _3_near), VISTA layer format.
Method: Fully procedural: dithered sunset gradient with striped sun and stars, ridge-noise mountains with pink rim, two generated neon skylines (random buildings, windows, antennas, sign bands). Made with tools/assets/pixel.py plus Python scripts. Not hand-drawn; props are drawn from coordinates in code.
Source: palette inspired by CC0 CityNight4Col 4-colour pack (no pixels copied)
Limits: Near and mid skylines look alike; windows are random dots; low variety.
Seam scores: see tools/assets/pixel.py tileable_score; checked at build time (all layers loop by construction, sparse sharp details raise the score a little).
quality: 3 of 5
Unity import (to do in inspector): point filter, no mipmaps, uncompressed, 16 px per unit.
